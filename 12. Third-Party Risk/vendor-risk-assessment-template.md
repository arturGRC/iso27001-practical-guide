## Assessment Questions
1. Does the vendor hold ISO 27001, SOC 2, or equivalent certification? [Y/N + evidence]
2. Does the vendor encrypt data at rest and in transit? [Y/N]
3. Does the vendor have a documented incident response process, and will they notify you of breaches? [Y/N + SLA]
4. Where is data hosted/processed (jurisdiction)? [relevant for GDPR/data residency]
5. Does the vendor subcontract to sub-processors? Are they disclosed?
6. Does the vendor perform background checks on staff with data access?

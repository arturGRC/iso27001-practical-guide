# Supplier Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Company registration / legal entity verified
- [ ] Financial stability check (for critical suppliers)
- [ ] Security certifications requested and validated (ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS as applicable)
- [ ] References checked
- [ ] Data Processing Agreement (DPA) reviewed/executed if personal data involved
- [ ] NDA executed before sensitive information shared
- [ ] Insurance coverage confirmed (cyber liability, as applicable)

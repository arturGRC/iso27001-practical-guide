# Risk Assessment Methodology

**Supports:** Clause 6.1, ISO 27005

## 1. Approach
Asset-based risk assessment: identify assets → identify threats/vulnerabilities → score likelihood x impact → determine treatment.

## 2. Scoring Scale
**Likelihood (1-5):** 1=Rare, 2=Unlikely, 3=Possible, 4=Likely, 5=Almost Certain
**Impact (1-5):** 1=Negligible, 2=Minor, 3=Moderate, 4=Major, 5=Severe (consider confidentiality, integrity, availability, legal/regulatory, financial, reputational)

**Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact** (range 1-25)

## 3. Risk Appetite / Acceptance Criteria
| Score Range | Rating | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low | Accept, monitor |
| 5-9 | Medium | Treat if cost-effective, or accept with owner sign-off |
| 10-15 | High | Must be treated; document treatment plan |
| 16-25 | Critical | Immediate action; escalate to top management |

## 4. Treatment Options
Mitigate (reduce likelihood/impact via controls) | Transfer (insurance, contract) | Avoid (discontinue the activity) | Accept (formal sign-off via Risk Acceptance Form)

## 5. Frequency
Full risk assessment performed annually; ad-hoc assessment triggered by major change, new system, or significant incident.

# 03. Risk Management

The engine of ISO 27001. Clause 6.1 requires a systematic approach to identifying, analyzing, and treating information security risk — this is what makes ISO 27001 "risk-based" rather than a fixed checklist.

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Risk Assessment Methodology | Defines your scoring scale (likelihood x impact), risk appetite, and acceptance criteria — do this before assessing any risk |
| Risk Register | Live inventory of identified risks, their scores, owners, and treatment status |
| Risk Treatment Plan | For each risk above appetite: the chosen treatment (mitigate/transfer/avoid/accept) and action plan |
| Risk Acceptance Form | Formal sign-off when a risk is knowingly accepted rather than treated |
| Asset Register | Information assets (data, systems, hardware) that risks are assessed against — you can't assess risk to what you haven't inventoried |

**Working template:** See `07. Templates/risk-assessment-template.csv` and `03. Risk Management/risk-register-template.csv` for ready-to-use spreadsheets aligned to ISO 27005.

# Interested Parties (Stakeholders)

**Supports:** Clause 4.2

| Interested Party | Requirements/Expectations | How Addressed |
|---|---|---|
| Customers | Data protection, uptime, breach notification | Contracts, SLAs, Information Security Policy |
| Employees | Safe handling of their personal data | HR privacy notices, Data Classification Policy |
| Regulators | Compliance with applicable law (GDPR, etc.) | Compliance folder, legal review |
| Shareholders/Board | Risk managed, reputation protected | Management Review, Risk Register |
| Suppliers/Partners | Clear security expectations | Vendor Security Policy, contracts |

# ISMS Objectives

**Supports:** Clause 6.2

| Objective | Metric | Target | Owner | Review Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reduce security incident impact | Mean time to contain critical incidents | < 4 hours | CISO | Quarterly |
| Improve patch compliance | % critical patches deployed within SLA | > 95% | IT Ops | Monthly |
| Build security culture | % staff completing annual awareness training | 100% | HR | Annual |
| Maintain access hygiene | % quarterly access reviews completed on time | 100% | IT Security | Quarterly |

Objectives are set consistent with the Information Security Policy, are measurable, communicated, and reviewed at Management Review.

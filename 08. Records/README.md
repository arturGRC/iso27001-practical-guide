# 08. Records

**Supports:** Clause 7.5 — "documented information required by the ISMS"

Records are the *evidence* that your ISMS is actually operating, not just documented. This is where auditors spend most of their time — policies show intent, records show execution.

| Subfolder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| Audit Reports | Completed internal/external audit reports and findings |
| Training Records | Completion logs for security awareness training |
| Incident Logs | Closed and open security incident records |
| Risk Assessments | Completed (dated) risk assessment outputs, vulnerability scan results |
| Supplier Assessments | Completed vendor due-diligence and review records |
| Management Reviews | Signed minutes from each management review meeting |
| Corrective Actions | Closed and open CARs from nonconformities |

**Note:** This is a public educational repo — do not commit real organizational data here. Use sanitized examples only, per `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

# Supplier Management Procedure

**Supports:** Vendor Security Policy | Controls A.5.19-A.5.22

## 1. Purpose
Operational steps for onboarding, monitoring, and offboarding suppliers with access to company data/systems.

## 2. Procedure
### 2.1 Onboarding
1. Supplier due diligence questionnaire completed (`12. Third-Party Risk/due-diligence-template.md`)
2. Risk tier assigned (Critical/High/Medium/Low) based on data access and service criticality
3. Security clauses and right-to-audit added to contract; NDA executed
4. Supplier added to Supplier Register with risk tier and review date

### 2.2 Ongoing Monitoring
1. Critical/High-tier suppliers reassessed annually (or on material change, e.g., breach disclosure)
2. Supplier security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) tracked and validated on renewal
3. Supplier-related incidents handled under Incident Management Procedure

### 2.3 Offboarding
1. Access revoked on contract end date
2. Data return/destruction confirmed in writing
3. Register updated to reflect closed relationship

## 3. Records
Assessments and reviews retained in `08. Records/Supplier Assessments`.

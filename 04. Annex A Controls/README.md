# 04. Annex A Controls

ISO 27001:2022 Annex A contains **93 controls** organized into 4 themes (this is the 2022 restructure — the 2013 version had 114 controls in 14 domains). Which controls apply to you is determined by your risk assessment and documented in the **Statement of Applicability (SoA)** — see `07. Templates`.

| Folder | Theme | Control count | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organizational Controls | A.5 | 37 controls | Policies, roles, supplier relationships, incident management |
| People Controls | A.6 | 8 controls | Screening, terms of employment, awareness, disciplinary process |
| Physical Controls | A.7 | 14 controls | Secure areas, equipment siting, clear desk/screen |
| Technological Controls | A.8 | 34 controls | Access control, cryptography, logging, malware protection |
| Control Implementation Evidence | — | — | Where you store proof each applicable control is actually operating (screenshots, configs, logs — cross-reference `14. Evidence`) |

**Common mistake:** Treating all 93 controls as mandatory. They are not — only the ones your risk assessment identifies as necessary are "applicable," and you must justify any exclusions in the SoA.

# 10. Internal Audit

**Supports:** Clause 9.2 — required at planned intervals to confirm the ISMS conforms to the standard and to your own requirements, and is effectively implemented and maintained.

| File/Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Audit Schedule | Rolling calendar of planned audits across the certification cycle |
| Audit Program | The overarching multi-year program (see `internal-audit-plan-template.md` in `07. Templates`) |
| Checklists | Completed checklists per audit (start from `07. Templates/internal-audit-checklist-template.csv`) |
| Findings | Nonconformities identified, feeding into Corrective Action Reports |
| Audit Reports | Final signed-off reports (also stored in `08. Records/Audit Reports`) |

**Auditor independence:** Per Clause 9.2, auditors must not audit their own work.

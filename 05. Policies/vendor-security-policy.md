# Vendor / Supplier Security Policy

**Supports:** Controls A.5.19, A.5.20, A.5.21, A.5.22

| Document Control | |
|---|---|
| Document Owner | [Procurement / CISO] |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Review Cycle | Annual |

## 1. Purpose
To manage information security risk arising from third-party suppliers and service providers.

## 2. Policy Statement
- All vendors with access to company data or systems undergo a security risk assessment before onboarding (`12. Third-Party Risk/vendor-risk-assessment-template.md`)
- Security requirements and right-to-audit clauses are included in contracts (Control A.5.20)
- Critical suppliers are reassessed at least annually
- Supplier access is provisioned per least privilege and revoked promptly on contract end
- Supply chain incidents involving a vendor are covered under the Incident Response Policy

## 3. Supplier Register
All active vendors are tracked in `12. Third-Party Risk/supplier-register-template.csv` with risk tier, data accessed, and last assessment date.

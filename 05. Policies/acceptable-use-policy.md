# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Supports:** Control A.5.10, A.6.2

| Document Control | |
|---|---|
| Document Owner | [HR / IT Security] |
| Version | 1.0 |
| Review Cycle | Annual |

## 1. Purpose
Defines acceptable and prohibited use of company information systems, devices, and networks.

## 2. Acceptable Use
- Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes; incidental personal use is permitted within reason
- Users must lock devices when unattended and follow the Password Policy

## 3. Prohibited Use
- Installing unauthorized software or bypassing security controls
- Accessing, storing, or transmitting illegal, offensive, or unlicensed content
- Sharing credentials or using another user's account
- Connecting unapproved personal devices to production networks without authorization
- Exfiltrating company or customer data to personal accounts/storage

## 4. Monitoring
Company systems may be monitored for security and compliance purposes in accordance with applicable law. Users have no expectation of privacy on company-owned systems.

## 5. Acknowledgement
All employees must sign the Employee Acknowledgement form (`09. Awareness/Employee Acknowledgement`) annually.

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines [Organization Name]'s commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets, and establishes the framework for setting information security objectives.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, third parties, and systems within the ISMS scope as defined in the Scope Document (`02. ISMS Documentation/scope-document.md`).

## 3. Policy Statement
[Organization Name] is committed to:
- Protecting information assets from threats, whether internal, external, deliberate, or accidental
- Ensuring business continuity and minimizing business damage
- Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements
- Continually improving the Information Security Management System (ISMS)
- Setting and reviewing measurable information security objectives (see ISMS Objectives)

## 4. Information Security Objectives
Objectives are defined, measured, and reviewed as part of management review (see `02. ISMS Documentation/isms-objectives.md`). Examples:
- Maintain zero critical security incidents causing >[X] hours of downtime
- Achieve [X]% completion of annual security awareness training
- Remediate critical vulnerabilities within [X] days of discovery

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Top Management | Approve policy, provide resources, demonstrate commitment |
| CISO / ISMS Manager | Own and maintain the ISMS, report to top management |
| Department Heads | Ensure compliance within their teams |
| All Employees | Comply with this policy and report security concerns |

## 6. Supporting Policies
This policy is supported by subordinate policies including Access Control, Acceptable Use, Password, Data Classification, Incident Response, Backup, Remote Work, Vendor Security, and Asset Management (see remaining files in this folder).

## 7. Non-Compliance
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, in accordance with the Employment Terms and applicable law.

## 8. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually and upon significant organizational or regulatory change, with approval recorded in Management Review Minutes.

## 1. Purpose
To ensure all information assets are identified, owned, inventoried, and appropriately protected throughout their lifecycle.

## 2. Policy Statement
- All information assets (hardware, software, data, cloud services) are recorded in the Asset Register (`03. Risk Management/asset-register-template.csv`)
- Every asset has a named owner accountable for its protection
- Assets are classified per the Data Classification Policy
- Assets are securely disposed of/wiped at end of life (Control A.7.14)
- Removable media use is restricted and logged where sensitive data is involved

## 3. Asset Lifecycle
Acquire → Register → Classify → Use → Maintain → Retire/Dispose, with the register updated at each stage.
